## Full description

```
Nouri is a simple, free calorie tracker. No adverts, no account, no subscriptions. Your food log stays on your phone.

Find your food fast
• Search basic foods from the USDA food database, from tortillas to roast chicken
• Find supermarket products from your own country with Open Food Facts
• Scan a barcode to fill in the nutrition from the label
• Tap a label serving, like "1 crumpet", instead of weighing everything

Log your day in seconds
• Recent foods remember the amount you had last time
• Save your usual meals and log them again in one tap
• See calories, protein, carbs and fat for every day, and look back at past days

Set a goal that fits you
• Get a suggested daily calorie goal from your height, weight, age and activity
• Choose to lose, maintain or gain weight at a steady pace
• Suggestions never go below commonly recommended minimums
• Nothing you type into the calculator is saved

Easy to read, your way
Nouri is designed with dyslexic readers in mind, following the British Dyslexia Association style guide:
• Tinted backgrounds (cream, yellow, blue, mint or peach) or dark mode
• Fonts including Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic
• Larger text, extra spacing, and an option to turn off capitals and italics

Private by design
• No account and no sign-up
• No adverts, no tracking and no analytics
• Your food log is stored only on your phone, and you can export a backup any time
• Searches only send the words you type, plus your country setting, to public food databases

Early version
Nouri is new and made by one independent developer. Use "Send feedback" in the app to say what's missing, broken or confusing.

Nouri gives estimates to help you keep track of what you eat. It is not medical advice.
```

Nouri privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version
  • There is no account, no adverts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Your food log stays on your phone. I can’t see it.
  • When you search for a food or look up a barcode, only the search words (plus your country setting) or the barcode number are sent to public food databases.
  • Nothing you type into the goal calculator is saved or sent anywhere.

Who makes Nouri

Nouri is a free calorie tracker made by John Goblin, an independent developer. In this policy, “I” means the developer and “the app” means Nouri.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these things in its own storage on your phone:

  • the foods you log, and the date for each one
  • your saved meals
  • your daily calorie goal, and the dates you changed it
  • your weight, if you choose to log it
  • your display settings, such as background colour, font, text size and weight unit

This information is never sent to me or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone’s settings, it is deleted.

Backups

The Export button makes a backup file of your log. You choose where it goes, for example Google Drive or an email to yourself. After that, the file is handled by the app or service you chose. The Import button only reads a backup file that you pick.

Food search

When you search for a food by name, the words you type are sent to FoodData Central, a free food database run by the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA). Nothing from your food log is sent. Like any website, USDA can see your phone’s internet (IP) address when the request arrives. See the USDA privacy policy.

In the Android app, the same search words are also sent to Open Food Facts (see below) to find supermarket products. To show products sold where you live first, the app also sends the country from your phone’s language and region settings, for example “United Kingdom”. It does not use your location.

Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free, non-profit product database based in France. Open Food Facts can see your phone’s IP address when the request arrives. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

The barcode scanner and your camera

The scanner is provided by Google Play services on your phone. It opens the camera and reads the barcode on your phone. Nouri never receives camera pictures, only the barcode number, and the app does not ask for permission to use your camera. Google may collect limited information about how the scanner works, under the ML Kit terms and the Google privacy policy.

The goal calculator

The calculator uses your age, height, weight, sex and activity level to suggest a daily calorie goal. These details are only used on screen while the calculator is open. They are never saved and never sent anywhere. Only the goal you choose to use is saved, on your phone.

Sending feedback

If you tap Send feedback, a Google Form opens. Whatever you type in the form is sent to me through Google Forms. If you leave the box ticked, the form also includes the app version and your phone’s make, model and Android version, to help me fix bugs. You can untick it. Please don’t include anything personal that you don’t want to share. I only use feedback to improve the app, and I’ll delete yours if you ask. Google Forms is covered by the Google privacy policy.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t show adverts.
  • I don’t use analytics or tracking tools.
  • I don’t sell or share your information.
  • I don’t ask you to create an account.

Where your information goes

USDA is in the United States, Open Food Facts is in France, and Google operates worldwide. Search words, your country setting, barcode numbers and feedback may be handled in those countries.

Children

Nouri is not made for children under 13. The goal calculator is for adults aged 18 and over.

Health information

Nouri gives estimates to help you keep track of what you eat. It is not medical advice. Please speak to a doctor before making big changes to your diet, especially if you are pregnant or have a health condition.

Your choices

  • To delete everything the app has stored, uninstall it or clear its storage in your phone’s settings.
  • To have your feedback deleted, email me at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
